Privacy & cookies policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

I respect your privacy. This page explains in plain words which of your data may be processed when you visit this website or contact me, why, and what your rights are under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Greek law.

1. Who is the data controller

Petros-Ioannis Tentolouris, Dietitian – Nutritionist, Trikala, Greece. Email: petrostentolouris@gmail.com.

2. This website does not store your data

The website has no forms that store data, uses no analytics or advertising tools and does not create visitor profiles.

The BMI and metabolic rate calculator runs entirely in your browser. The details you enter (age, height, weight, etc.) are not sent or stored by me or the website.

3. What data I process and why

Contact by email. If you email me, I receive your name, email address and whatever you write. I use them only to reply to you or handle your request. Legal basis: steps you request before a possible collaboration and my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Booking through Calendly. The booking button opens Calendly in a new tab, where you enter your name and email to schedule the free introductory call or a session. This data is used to schedule and manage appointments. Legal basis: steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Calendly’s own privacy policy also applies.

Reviews. If you send me a review by email, I process the name you choose to display and the text of your review. Reviews are not published automatically: I read them first and publish them only if you have stated that you agree. The name appears as you chose it (e.g. first name and surname initial). I do not publish your email address or any other personal details the message may contain. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by email; the review is then removed from the website.

Technical data. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically log technical data (e.g. IP address, time of visit, browser type) for the secure operation and protection of the site. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in the security and proper operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Health data

Please do not send medical tests, medical history or other health information by email or Calendly before our collaboration starts.

During nutritional follow-up I may need to collect and process data concerning your health, such as medical history, test results, medication, body measurements and eating habits. This data is used solely to assess your nutritional needs, provide personalised dietary guidance and monitor your progress.

Health data is subject to the special provisions of Article 9 GDPR, together with the appropriate legal basis under Article 6. Before we start working together you will receive separate information about how your data is processed and protected.

5. Who the data is shared with

I do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising. Data may be processed by providers that are necessary to run the website and communication:

  • the website hosting provider,
  • the email provider (Google),
  • Calendly (appointment booking).

These providers process data as part of their services and in line with their own terms and contractual commitments.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Where such transfers take place, the appropriate GDPR safeguards apply, such as the adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider participates in it, or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long I keep data

I keep simple contact emails for as long as needed to complete our communication and, as a rule, up to 12 months after the last contact, unless we start working together or there is another lawful reason to keep them.

Published reviews remain on the website until you ask for them to be removed. Reviews that are not published are deleted within a reasonable time.

Data relating to nutritional follow-up and tax records are kept for as long as required by the nature of the collaboration and applicable professional and tax obligations.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request:

  • access to your data,
  • rectification,
  • erasure, where the legal conditions are met,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability, where applicable,
  • to object to processing, where provided,
  • to withdraw your consent, where processing is based on it.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

For any of these, email me at petrostentolouris@gmail.com. I will reply within one month of receiving your request at the latest, which may be extended where the GDPR allows.

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

8. Minors

This website is not aimed at children. If a collaboration concerns a minor, communication about starting and managing it is handled by the parent or legal guardian, in accordance with applicable law.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website.

This website does not use its own analytics, tracking or advertising cookies and does not track visitors for advertising.

Third-party services, such as Calendly when you open it in a new tab or Instagram when you go to it, may use their own cookies and similar technologies under their own policies.

If cookies or other technologies requiring consent are added to the website in the future, your consent will be requested before they are enabled. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

10. Changes

If anything changes in how the website works or how personal data is processed, I will update this page and the date at the top.
