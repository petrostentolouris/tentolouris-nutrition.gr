My method

Nutrition, scientifically structured,personally tailored.

Nutrition built around you, your habits, your needs and your way of life.

Starting point: you

I start from what is already part of your everyday life: your eating habits, your preferences, your needs and what makes you unique. Based on these, I create a personalised plan that evolves gradually with you.

Progress, step by step

With ongoing follow-up we review your progress and make, step by step, the adjustments you need, with no unnecessary restrictions and no rigid rules.

How I work

It all starts with an introductory call: you tell me what concerns you and what you want to achieve. It is completely free and there is no commitment.
If we decide to work together, our journey has 4 steps!

  1. We understand your daily life

    In our first online session I get to know in depth what you eat, how you organise your meals, your preferences and your needs.

  2. We create your own plan

    A personalised plan based on your real habits, without having to turn your daily life upside down.

  3. We improve gradually

    We don’t change everything on day one. We move step by step, with small, meaningful changes.

  4. We grow together

    In our weekly sessions we review your progress, talk through any difficulties and adjust the plan.
