About me

Twice sworn to the Hippocratic Oath, to protect your health.

I’m Petros-Ioannis Tentolouris, a Dietitian – Nutritionist from Trikala, Greece. I believe that real change in nutrition is not just about reaching a goal or sticking to a meal plan. It is about understanding nutrition, building healthy habits and creating a balanced relationship with food.

Through personalised guidance and ongoing support, my aim is to give you the knowledge and skills to make mindful choices that last in the long run. My approach is built on scientific evidence, personalisation and respect for each person’s daily life.
