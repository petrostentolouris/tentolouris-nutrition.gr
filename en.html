My method

Your nutrition doesn’t need to start from scratch.

I start from what is already part of your everyday life: your eating habits, preferences, needs and particularities.

Based on these, I create a personalised meal plan tailored to your lifestyle. Not a ready-made diet, but a plan that evolves gradually with you.

Through ongoing follow-up we review your progress and make the adjustments you need, step by step, without unnecessary deprivation or rigid rules.

More than a meal plan.

The goal is for you to gain the knowledge, skills and independence to manage your eating with confidence and balance.

About me

Twice sworn to the Hippocratic Oath, to protect your health.

I’m Petros-Ioannis Tentolouris, a Dietitian – Nutritionist from Trikala, Greece, with a master’s degree in “Clinical Dietetics – Nutrition in Health and Disease” from the School of Medicine, University of Thessaly, completed with distinction.

I believe real change in nutrition isn’t just about reaching a goal or following a meal plan. It’s about understanding nutrition, building healthy habits and creating a balanced relationship with food.

Through personalised guidance and ongoing support, my aim is to help you gain the knowledge and skills you need to make mindful food choices and keep them up in the long term.

My approach is based on scientific evidence, personalisation and respect for each person’s needs and everyday life.

Frequently asked questions

How do online sessions work?+

Sessions take place by video call, from your computer, tablet or phone, wherever you are. All you need is an internet connection and a quiet space for us to talk.

Is the first call free?+

Yes! Our first introductory call lasts 15 minutes, is completely free and doesn’t commit you to working together. It’s a chance to discuss your goals and needs, get to know each other and see if we’re a good fit.

Do I need to live in Trikala?+

No! Sessions are entirely online, so we can work together wherever you are. Whether you live in Trikala, any other city in Greece or abroad, you can get personalised nutrition support from the comfort of your own space.

How often will we meet?+

Our work is based on weekly online sessions, as part of a monthly follow-up programme. Each week we review your progress, discuss any difficulties and questions, and adjust your meal plan where needed, aiming for steady, meaningful progress.
