Frequently asked questions

How do the online sessions work?

Sessions take place by video call, from your computer, tablet or phone, wherever you are. All you need is an internet connection and a quiet place to talk.

Is the first call free?

Yes. Our first call lasts 15 minutes, is completely free and does not commit you to working together.

Do I need to live in Trikala?

No. Sessions are held entirely online, so we can work together whether you live in Trikala, anywhere else in Greece or abroad.

How often will we meet?

We work together through weekly online sessions, as part of a monthly follow-up programme.
